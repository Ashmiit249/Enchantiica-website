Orders & delivery

How long will my order take to arrive?

We dispatch within 1–2 working days. UK standard delivery then takes 2–4 working days and express takes 1–2. International orders typically arrive within 5–14 working days. See our delivery page for full details.

Do you offer free delivery?

Yes — UK delivery is free on orders over £50. Under £50, standard delivery is £3.50.

Can I track my order?

Every order is sent tracked. You’ll receive a tracking link by email as soon as your parcel is on its way.

Can I change or cancel my order?

If your order hasn’t been dispatched yet, absolutely. Contact us as soon as possible with your order number and we’ll do our best.

Do you ship internationally?

We do. We ship to Europe, the USA, Canada, Australia and most other countries. Duties and taxes for orders outside the UK are the responsibility of the customer.

Is my order gift-wrapped?

Every order arrives in a blush gift box or cotton pouch with a printed meaning card, at no extra cost. You can add a handwritten note in the notes box at checkout, and we never include prices in the parcel.

Crystals & care

Are your crystals genuine?

Yes. Every stone is natural and sourced from small, trusted suppliers. Because they’re natural, colour, pattern and size vary — no two are identical, and yours will differ slightly from the photos.

Are your crystals cleansed before they arrive?

Every piece is cleansed with sound and charged under the full moon before dispatch. Many customers like to cleanse again on arrival to make the piece their own — a few deep breaths and a clear intention is enough.

How do I cleanse and charge my crystals?
  • Selenite: lay your crystals or jewellery on a selenite wand overnight.
  • Moonlight: place them on a windowsill under a full moon.
  • Sound: a singing bowl, bell or even a favourite song.
  • Avoid water for soft or porous stones such as Selenite, Malachite and Lepidolite.
Which crystal should I start with?

Trust what you’re drawn to — that’s usually the right one. If you’d like a guide, our Beginner’s Crystal Kit includes seven essentials and an illustrated booklet, or browse by intention in the shop.

Are crystals a substitute for medical care?

No. We love crystals as a mindful way to set intentions and feel supported, but they are not a substitute for medical, psychological or professional advice. Please always seek help from a qualified professional when you need it.

Jewellery & sizing

What are your jewellery pieces made of?

All findings, chains and settings are 18ct gold-plated sterling silver — nickel-free and gentle on sensitive skin. Bead bracelets are strung on a strong, replaceable stretch cord.

How do I find my ring size?

Wrap a strip of paper around the base of your finger, mark where it overlaps and measure the length in millimetres. Then use the guide below. If you’re between sizes, size up.

UK sizeCircumferenceUS size
J48.7 mm5
L51.2 mm6
N53.8 mm7
P56.3 mm8
R58.9 mm9
How do I choose a bracelet size?

Measure snugly around your wrist bone with a tape measure or a strip of paper. Small fits 15–16 cm, Medium 17–18 cm and Large 19–20 cm. Most people find Medium is right.

How should I care for gold-plated jewellery?
  • Take pieces off before showering, swimming or exercising.
  • Put jewellery on last, after perfume and lotions.
  • Store in the pouch provided, away from other pieces.
  • Wipe gently with a soft dry cloth — no chemical cleaners.
Can I get a longer chain?

Most pendants come in a choice of 16", 18" and 20" lengths. If you’d like something different, get in touch and we’ll see what we can do.

Returns & exchanges

What is your returns policy?

You can return unworn, unused items in their original packaging within 30 days of delivery for a refund or exchange. For hygiene reasons, earrings can’t be returned unless faulty. Read the full returns policy.

My item arrived damaged — what do I do?

We’re so sorry. Please contact us within 48 hours of delivery with a photo and your order number, and we’ll send a replacement or refund straight away.

Can I exchange for a different size?

Yes. Send the piece back unworn within 30 days and we’ll swap it for the size you need, free of charge for UK customers.

How long do refunds take?

Once your return arrives we process it within 3 working days. Refunds then take 3–5 working days to show, depending on your bank.
