Privacy policy

Enchantiica (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use this website or place an order, how we use it and the choices you have. We process personal data in line with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Information we collect

  • Order details: your name, delivery address, email address, phone number and the items you buy, so we can fulfil and support your order.
  • Payment information: handled entirely by our payment provider. We never see or store your full card details.
  • Messages: anything you send us via the contact form, email or Instagram.
  • Newsletter sign-ups: your email address, if you choose to subscribe.
  • Usage data: basic, anonymised analytics about how the site is used, such as pages visited and device type.

How we use it

  • To process, deliver and provide customer service for your orders (contractual necessity).
  • To send order confirmations, dispatch notifications and tracking updates.
  • To send our newsletter, only if you’ve opted in — you can unsubscribe with one click at any time (consent).
  • To improve the site and our range (legitimate interests).
  • To meet legal obligations such as accounting and tax records.

Who we share it with

We only share your information with the services we need to run the shop: our payment provider, delivery carriers, our email platform and our website host. Each is contractually required to protect your data. We never sell your information.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept for 6 years to meet UK accounting rules. Newsletter data is kept until you unsubscribe. Contact messages are deleted 12 months after the conversation ends.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, restrict or object to how we use it, or receive a copy in a portable format. To exercise any of these rights, contact us. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).

Cookies & local storage

This site uses your browser’s local storage to remember the items in your bag between visits. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us. We use only essential cookies and privacy-friendly, anonymised analytics; we don’t use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. You can clear local storage and cookies at any time in your browser settings.
