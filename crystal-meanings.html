Crystal guide

Crystal meanings & benefits

Every stone we sell, what it is traditionally used for, and how to work with it. Trust what you’re drawn to — that’s usually the right one.

Start with a feeling

Pick the stone whose benefits match what you need right now — calm, courage, love, focus. You can also shop by intention.

Keep it close

Crystals work best where you’ll see or touch them: in a pocket, on a desk, under a pillow or worn as jewellery.

Cleanse now and then

Lay stones on selenite overnight, leave them in moonlight or use sound. Keep soft stones like Selenite dry. See our care guide.

Rose Quartz

Rose Quartz is the stone of unconditional love. Gentle and soothing, it is traditionally used to open the heart, encourage self-compassion and ease emotional wounds.

  • Encourages self-love and compassion
  • Soothes emotional wounds and heartache
  • Invites gentle, loving energy into relationships
  • Calms stress and supports restful sleep

How to use it: Keep it close when you need a little tenderness — on your bedside table, in your pocket, or worn over the heart.
