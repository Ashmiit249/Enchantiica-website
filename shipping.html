Shipping & delivery

Every order is packed by hand and dispatched within 1–2 working days, tracked, in plastic-free packaging.

Free delivery across the UAE on every order over AED 250. No code needed — it’s applied automatically in your bag.

Delivery estimates start from the day your order is dispatched, not the day it’s placed. Working days are Monday to Friday, excluding UAE public holidays.

Processing times

We pack orders Monday to Friday. Orders placed before 12pm (Gulf Standard Time) are usually dispatched the same day; everything else goes out within 1–2 working days. During launches and the festive season this may stretch to 3 working days — we’ll always let you know on the shop and on Instagram if so.

Cash on delivery

For orders delivered within the UAE you can choose cash on delivery at checkout. A AED 10 handling fee is added, and the courier will accept cash or card at your door. Cash-on-delivery orders that are refused or cannot be delivered after two attempts are returned to us, and the delivery fee is not refundable.

Tracking your order

All orders are sent tracked. You’ll get a dispatch email with your tracking link as soon as your parcel is on its way. If nothing has arrived after the estimated window, check the tracking link first, then contact us with your order number and we’ll chase it for you.

International orders, duties & taxes

Orders shipped outside the UAE may be subject to import duties, taxes and handling fees charged by your country. These are set by your local customs office and are the responsibility of the recipient. We mark every parcel accurately and can’t mark orders as gifts.

Packaging

Every order is wrapped in tissue paper inside a recycled gift box or cotton pouch, with a printed meaning card. Boxes are sealed with paper tape and posted in recycled mailers — nothing in your parcel needs to go to landfill. Fragile pieces such as clusters, spheres and towers are wrapped in extra padding.

Something not right?

If your parcel arrives damaged, or something is missing, please get in touch within 48 hours with a photo and your order number. We’ll sort it straight away with a replacement or a refund.

Looking for our returns policy? Unworn items can be returned within 30 days of delivery.
