Help

Returns policy

We want you to love what you receive. If something isn’t right, you have 30 days from delivery to return it.

Our promise

Every piece leaves our studio checked, cleansed and carefully wrapped. If it isn’t right for you, return it within 30 days of delivery for a refund or exchange — no awkward questions.

What can be returned

  • Items must be unworn and unused, in their original packaging with the meaning card and pouch or box.
  • Crystals should be returned in the condition they arrived in — please don’t return a piece that has been carried, cleansed in water or displayed for a while.
  • For hygiene reasons, earrings cannot be returned or exchanged unless faulty.
  • Personalised or made-to-order pieces can only be returned if faulty.
  • Sale items can be returned for an exchange or store credit.

How to start a return

  1. Tell us

    Contact us with your order number and the item you’d like to return. We’ll reply with your return reference and address within one working day.

  2. Pack it up

    Wrap the item securely in its original packaging with your return reference inside. Fragile crystals need extra padding.

  3. Send it back

    Within the UAE we arrange a courier collection for exchanges free of charge. For refunds and for orders outside the UAE, send it back with a tracked service and keep your proof of postage — return shipping is the customer’s responsibility unless the item is faulty.

  4. Refund or exchange

    We process returns within 3 working days of receiving them. Refunds take a further 3–5 working days to reach your account.

Faulty or damaged items

If your order arrives damaged or develops a fault within 6 months, please get in touch with a photo and your order number. We’ll arrange a free replacement or full refund, including any postage you’ve paid. Normal wear and tear — such as plating fading after contact with water or perfume — isn’t considered a fault, but we’re always happy to advise on care.

Exchanges

Need a different size or chain length? Let us know when you start your return and we’ll hold the replacement for you. UAE exchanges are collected and re-delivered free of charge.

Refunds

Refunds are made to the original payment method; cash-on-delivery orders are refunded by bank transfer or store credit, whichever you prefer. Original delivery charges are refunded only when an item is faulty or we’ve made a mistake with your order. Promotional discounts are deducted proportionally.

This policy doesn’t affect your rights under UAE Federal Law No. 15 of 2020 on Consumer Protection.
