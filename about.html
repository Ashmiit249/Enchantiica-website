About us

Small shop, big intention

Enchantiica started with one rose quartz and a kitchen table. It still runs on the same belief: the things we keep close should mean something.

The Enchantiica studio — crystals laid out on linenImage placeholder
How it began

From a kitchen table to your doorstep

Enchantiica began as a handful of tumblestones and a few pendants photographed on a windowsill for Instagram. The first orders were wrapped in tissue at a kitchen table in Dubai, each with a handwritten note about the stone inside.

Word spread the way it does when something feels genuine. Today we deliver across the UAE, the Gulf and beyond, but every order is still packed by hand, cleansed before it goes out and sent with a little card explaining what your crystal is for.

We’re not here to promise miracles. Crystals, for us, are a beautiful way to set an intention and carry it with you — a small, tangible reminder of how you want to feel.

What we stand for

Our values

Ethically sourced

We buy from small, trusted suppliers who can tell us where each stone was mined and how the people who mined it are treated.

Cleansed & charged

Every piece is cleansed with sound and charged under the full moon before dispatch, so it arrives clear and ready to work with.

Made in small batches

Our jewellery is hand-finished in small runs, which means fewer pieces, closer attention and no two exactly alike.

Plastic-free packaging

Recycled boxes, cotton pouches, paper tape and tissue. Nothing in your parcel needs to go to landfill.

A note from the founder

A note from the founder

I started Enchantiica during a difficult year, when a single piece of rose quartz on my bedside table became a small daily reminder to be gentler with myself. I wanted to share that feeling — not as a cure, but as a comfort.

Every stone we send out is one I would happily keep. Thank you for letting us be a small part of your rituals.

With love,Founder, Enchantiica

From our hands to yours

How every order is made

  1. Chosen by hand

    We select each stone for colour, clarity and feel. Anything that doesn’t make us smile doesn’t make it in.

  2. Cleansed & charged

    Stones are cleansed with sound and left to charge under the full moon before they’re packed.

  3. Wrapped with intention

    Each piece is wrapped in tissue with a printed meaning card and, if you like, a handwritten note.

  4. On its way

    Orders are dispatched within 1–2 working days in recycled, plastic-free packaging.
